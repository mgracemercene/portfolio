# Transaction Review & Exception Log

## Objective
Turn unclear or unusual transactions into traceable questions and actionable follow-ups.

| Ref | Example issue | Review action | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| T-001 | Receipt not attached | Request receipt and business purpose | Pending |
| T-002 | Vendor description unclear | Confirm vendor and account coding | Pending |
| T-003 | Possible duplicate payment | Compare date, amount, payee, and bank reference | Under review |
| T-004 | Bank amount differs from book entry | Trace source document and correct only after verification | Pending |

## Review principles
- Do not guess the business purpose of a transaction.
- Keep source documents and notes connected to the transaction.
- Distinguish confirmed errors from items needing client clarification.
- Record who needs to respond and when follow-up is due.
- Preserve an audit trail for any correction.

*All references above are fictional examples.*
