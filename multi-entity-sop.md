# Multi-Entity Bookkeeping SOP Framework

## Purpose
Provide a repeatable structure for supporting multiple legal entities without mixing their financial activity.

## Entity setup
Maintain a separate entity profile containing:
- Legal business name and tax/entity details (stored securely outside this public portfolio)
- Accounting basis and fiscal year
- QuickBooks Online company file
- Approved chart of accounts
- Bank, credit-card, loan, and payment accounts
- Payroll and sales-tax responsibilities, where applicable
- Reporting deadlines and approval contacts

## Operating controls
1. Confirm the active entity before entering or coding transactions.
2. Use entity-specific vendor, customer, and account mappings.
3. Record intercompany activity only with supporting documentation and matching entries.
4. Reconcile each entity independently.
5. Escalate unclear owner transactions, transfers, and shared expenses.
6. Restrict access based on role and business need.

## Weekly and monthly rhythm
- Weekly: transaction review, AP/AR follow-up, missing-document requests, and status update.
- Monthly: reconciliations, balance-sheet review, close checklist, and reporting package.

## Communication standard
Send concise updates covering completed work, exceptions, decisions needed, responsible person, and target date.
