# AI-Assisted Workflow Improvement

## Goal
Use AI to reduce repetitive drafting and documentation effort while keeping accounting decisions under human control.

## Example workflow
1. Define a repetitive task, its inputs, expected output, and risk.
2. Remove or mask confidential information before using an AI tool.
3. Ask AI to organize information, draft a checklist, or propose a workflow.
4. Verify every accounting fact, calculation, account mapping, and recommendation against source records and company policy.
5. Have an authorized person approve changes before posting or communicating externally.
6. Document the final process and measure time saved, error rates, and review effort.

## Appropriate uses
- Drafting follow-up messages for missing receipts.
- Creating first-draft SOPs and checklists.
- Summarizing non-sensitive process notes.
- Structuring exception logs and review questions.

## Guardrails
- Never upload credentials, bank details, tax IDs, payroll data, or identifiable client records to unapproved tools.
- AI output is a draft, not an accounting authority.
- Do not allow AI to post transactions, change vendor banking details, or send client communications without authorized review.
- Keep a human reviewer accountable for final decisions.
