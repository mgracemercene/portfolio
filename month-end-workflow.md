# Month-End Bookkeeping Workflow

## Objective
Create a consistent close process that improves completeness, accuracy, and visibility before financial reports are shared.

## Example workflow
1. Collect bank, credit-card, loan, and payroll statements.
2. Review bank-feed transactions and supporting documents.
3. Categorize income and expenses using the approved chart of accounts and vendor rules.
4. Review bills, invoices, payments, deposits, and transfers for duplicates or incorrect coding.
5. Reconcile each balance-sheet account to its supporting statement or schedule.
6. Investigate unreconciled items and document open questions.
7. Review profit and loss and balance sheet for unusual movements.
8. Prepare a concise close summary, outstanding-items list, and next steps.

## Quality checks
- Reconciliation difference is zero or clearly explained.
- Duplicate and uncategorized transactions are reviewed.
- Loan principal and interest are treated according to the loan schedule and accounting policy.
- Owner activity is reviewed using the entity's approved treatment.
- Open questions have an owner and follow-up date.

## Deliverables
- Reconciliation status tracker
- Exception and missing-document log
- Month-end close checklist
- Draft financial reports and management notes

*Demonstration framework only; adapt to each client's accounting policy and reviewer requirements.*
